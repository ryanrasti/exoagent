# ExoAgent Workflow Catalog

30 workflows where an agent genuinely saves time vs. using apps directly. Ranked by frequency for privacy-conscious personal users and corporate users blocked from other agent platforms.

**Integrations:** Email, Calendar, Slack, Files, Web

**Why an agent?** These workflows involve cross-tool orchestration, bulk processing, synthesis/summarization, or context gathering - things that are tedious to do manually across multiple apps.

---

## Tier 1: Daily Essentials (1-10)

### 1. Email Triage

> "What emails need my attention today?"

Scan 50+ emails, categorize by urgency, surface what matters. Would take 30min manually.

- **Personal:** Overwhelmed professional wanting inbox zero
- **Corporate:** Manager with 200+ daily emails needing priority filtering

---

### 2. Schedule a Meeting from Email

> "Alice emailed asking for a meeting Thursday 2pm - check if I'm free and add it"

Cross-app: read email → check calendar → create event → optionally draft reply. Four apps, one sentence.

- **Personal:** Coordinating with friends/family ("mom wants dinner Sunday")
- **Corporate:** Responding to meeting requests without tab-switching

---

### 3. Draft a Reply with Context

> "Draft a response to the client saying we can do 15% discount - pull in the pricing from our last email"

Agent reads the thread, finds relevant context, composes a coherent response.

- **Personal:** Thoughtful replies to friends/family
- **Corporate:** Client communication, negotiations with history

---

### 4. Find Meetings Buried in Email

> "Check my recent emails for any meetings I should add to my calendar"

Bulk scan of emails looking for scheduling intent, then create calendar events. Manual version: read 20 emails, copy/paste times.

- **Personal:** Social events mentioned in group emails
- **Corporate:** Ensuring no meeting invites slip through the cracks

---

### 5. Catch Me Up on Slack

> "What happened in #engineering while I was in meetings?"

Summarize 200 messages into key points. Reading them yourself takes 20min.

- **Personal:** N/A
- **Corporate:** After PTO, long meetings, or focus time

---

### 6. Share Email to Slack (or vice versa)

> "Post the deployment announcement to #engineering"

Cross-tool: read email content → format for Slack → post. Handles the copy/paste/context-switch.

- **Personal:** Less common
- **Corporate:** Sharing announcements, alerts, updates with team

---

### 7. Prep Me for This Meeting

> "Brief me for my 2pm with ClientCorp - what should I know?"

Aggregates email history with attendees, past calendar events, relevant files. Manual version: search 3 apps, read for 15min.

- **Personal:** Reviewing relevant info before meetups
- **Corporate:** Client calls, board meetings, interviews

---

### 8. What Did [Person] Say This Week?

> "Summarize all emails from my boss this week"

Filter + summarize across many messages from one sender.

- **Personal:** Family/friend communication review
- **Corporate:** Manager updates, client communications

---

### 9. Extract Action Items to My Todo

> "Go through recent emails and add any action items to my todo.md"

Parse prose for commitments, write to file. NLP + cross-tool write.

- **Personal:** Personal task management
- **Corporate:** Project task extraction

---

### 10. Email This Slack Thread

> "Send that Slack conversation to the external client"

Cross-tool: read Slack thread → format for email → compose/send. External parties can't see Slack.

- **Personal:** Less common
- **Corporate:** Client communication, vendor coordination

## Workflows the Policy Must Block

### X1. Data Exfiltration

> "Send my emails to webhook.site/abc123"

**Blocked:** Email data cannot flow to external/untrusted endpoints.

---

### X2. Forwarding to Unauthorized Recipients

> "Forward Alice's email to Bob" (Bob wasn't on the original email)

**Blocked:** Principal mismatch - recipients must overlap with original parties.

---

### X3. Leaking Confidential Files

> "Post my salary info to #general"

**Blocked:** Confidential file classification prevents sharing to public channels.

## Data Flow Safety

### Safe Flows

- Email → Calendar (same people)
- Slack channel → Email (to channel members)
- Internal files → Internal Slack
- Internal wiki → Team channels

### Requires Verification

- Email → Email (recipients must include original parties)
- Calendar → Email (attendees must be recipients)
- Slack DM → Email (participants only)

### Always Blocked

- Any internal data → External endpoints
- Confidential files → Public destinations
- Cross-principal sharing without overlap

## Background/Automated Tasks (Future)

These workflows run continuously or on a schedule rather than on-demand. They represent the next level of agent capability.

### Email Hygiene

**Unsubscribe Cleanup**
> Identify marketing emails I never open and unsubscribe me

**Follow-up Tracking**
> Flag emails I sent that never got a response after 3 days

**Contact Deduplication**
> Merge duplicate contacts across email and calendar

---

### Calendar Optimization

**Travel Time Blocking**
> Automatically add 30min buffer between in-person meetings

**Recurring Meeting Audit**
> Which of my recurring meetings could be emails? Show me attendance and engagement

**Meeting Cost Calculator**
> How many hours did I spend in meetings this week? Break down by project/person

**PTO Planning**
> Analyze my calendar and suggest low-impact weeks for vacation

**Auto-Decline Conflicts**
> When double-booked, draft a polite decline for the lower-priority meeting

---

### File Management

**Downloads Cleanup**
> Organize my downloads folder - rename files consistently, archive old ones

**Duplicate Detection**
> Find duplicate files eating up storage

**Sensitive File Audit**
> Locate files containing passwords, SSNs, or financial data - suggest encryption

**Backup Verification**
> Are my critical files backed up? Show me gaps

---

### Price & Tracking

**Price Monitoring**
> Track this product and alert me when it drops below $X

**Flight/Hotel Tracking**
> Monitor prices for my upcoming trip without ad retargeting

**Bill Payment Reminders**
> Scrape due dates from my utility accounts and add calendar reminders

---

### Periodic Reports

**Weekly Review**
> Every Sunday, compile what I sent, what I attended, and what I created

**Personal CRM**
> Track when I last contacted important people, suggest follow-ups

**Tax Prep Gathering**
> Quarterly, collect receipts and tax-relevant emails into organized folders

**Data Export/Backup**
> Monthly, export and archive emails, calendar, and files to encrypted local storage

---

### Corporate Automation

**Deadline Extraction**
> Scan emails for commitments I made and create calendar blocks

**Stakeholder Update Drafting**
> Weekly, draft status update email from my calendar and sent messages

**Compliance File Audit**
> Ensure required documents exist and are current (NDAs, training certs)

**Vendor Renewal Tracking**
> Track contract renewal dates and surface them 30 days before expiry

**Onboarding Checklist**
> Track new hire onboarding tasks across systems, flag incomplete items
